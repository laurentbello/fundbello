Who is worth cloning

Each manager is scored on the filing record alone, as of Q3 2026: does the book hold together over time, how concentrated is it, and do they add or cut when they act. Returns are left out on purpose — filings cannot measure them, and a high score here means “the positions can be studied”, not “the positions will work”. The tier is an editorial call layered on top.

Clone
Long-hold, valuation-disciplined books whose positions are worth studying as candidates.
Read-only
Good signal on what quality managers own, but the book is shaped by something other than moat-and-price.
Trader
Macro, event-driven or activist books; the 13F is a trading snapshot, not a list of things to own.
